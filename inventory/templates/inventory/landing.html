Pharmacy FEFO Engine

A smart, First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO) inventory management system designed to guarantee zero expired medicines are ever dispensed.

Target Audience

Neighbourhood pharmacies, clinics, and regional hospitals that manage time-sensitive medical stock and need automated dispensing compliance.

How It Helps

It automatically isolates expired stock and strictly routes dispensing requests to the oldest valid batches first, protecting patient safety and reducing financial waste.

Key Features Included

  • Automated FEFO (First-Expiry-First-Out) Dispensing Logic.
  • Real-time isolation of expired batches from sellable stock.
  • Complete REST APIs with Search, Sorting, and Pagination.
  • Role-based user registration and authentication.

Next 3 Features We Will Build

  1. Automated Expiry Alerts: Email/SMS notifications for stock expiring within 30 days.
  2. Data Analytics Dashboard: Visualizing monthly consumption trends and stock bottlenecks.
  3. Barcode Scanner Integration: For rapid batch entry and real-time dispensing updates.
